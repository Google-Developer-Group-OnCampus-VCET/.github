## About Us

GDG OnCampus VCET is a student-driven developer community that brings together technology enthusiasts to explore new technologies, build solutions, and collaborate on innovative ideas.

## What We Do

- Organize technical workshops and hands-on sessions.
- Build projects and explore emerging technologies.
- Participate in hackathons and coding events.
- Encourage open-source contributions and collaboration.
- Create opportunities for students to learn and grow.
